## Process, authority and evidence boundaries

The two phases use different real native PIDs. The controller sends SIGKILL to
the warm native process only after the warm method has passed and all five warm
ledger rows are validated. The cold native process inherits the unchanged OS
TCP and UDP denial filter, starts with zero peers, reads all six claims and the
body exactly, and retries saved A without changing its operation identity.

Each write intent is recorded durably before dispatch. An unknown or unexpected
outcome remains unqualified and is not automatically retried. The final ledger
requires five acknowledged and validated write calls and two confirmed retired
lease refusals. All four owned native/Swift process groups must be positively
absent; only the designated native crash may have exit status -SIGKILL.

The current qualified test products must match the component receipt before
each `--skip-build` launch and remain byte equal afterward. Source files, the
original archive, original semantic fixture, native source manifest and native
binary must remain unchanged. The already accepted network-denial probe is
reused by exact hash; the new processes independently verify their inherited
NoNewPrivs and Seccomp state.

development floor. The private account is only 610,070 logical bytes; a bounded
128 MiB root allowance includes copy rounding, WAL, logs, proof files, temporary
runner work and slack. Explicit account, log, proof and whole-output caps remain
active during the run. No original accounts or unique evidence are removed to
obtain capacity.
